Core rules

The short version. The full rulebook lives in the Discord.

No RDM or VDM. Don't kill or run people over without a roleplay reason.
Value your life. Act like your character would in real danger. No fail roleplay.
No tool abuse. Items and tools are for roleplay, not for trolling other players.
New life rule. After you die, forget the events that led to your death.
Respect staff. Follow staff instructions in game. Take disputes to a ticket.
